Pools of the Deep VR

A liminal explorer...

Introduction:

You will explore carefully created liminal spaces that emotionally connect with the surroundings. There is no need to rush, there is no stress. You play it at your own pace. Walk, swim, explore. In this eerie and surreal adventure you will collect puzzle pieces scattered through the levels.

Environments:

The project will have around 6 levels, each with a distinct style. Right now I have the first level completed (Classic baths inspired). The second one (Ancient Egyptian tomb inspired) is on its way and soon will be baked and tested.

Game play:

One of the main ideas of this game is to create a eerie / surreal feeling in the player. Sense of being alone, and discovery. The player has some tools such as a Bow/Arrow and a lamp. The player can shoot with his arch several things, the floating puzzle pieces and open doors.

Platform:

The game will be initially launched as a PCVR game on Steam. There is the possibility to transform the game into a non VR flat PC game. Another possibility is to convert it into a Standalone project for Quest / Frame headsets and other VR platforms.
